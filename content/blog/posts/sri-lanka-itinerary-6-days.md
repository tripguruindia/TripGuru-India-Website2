Six days works if you keep moving and use a private driver. This plan links the Temple of the Sacred Tooth in Kandy, tea country around Nuwara Eliya and the Madu River and beaches near Bentota, with Ramayana sites along the way. It suits families, couples and pilgrim groups who want variety without rushing.

## Do Indians need a visa or ETA for Sri Lanka?

You need an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) before you travel. Apply online at eta.gov.lk. The official site lists India among the nationalities that currently get the tourist ETA free of charge, for a stay of 30 days with double entry allowed within those 30 days. It covers ordinary Indian passports.

Apply only through the official portal, eta.gov.lk.

Processing is usually quick, with some reports saying about 24–48 hours. Check eta.gov.lk and apply well ahead rather than the night before. Confirm passport validity and onward-ticket requirements on eta.gov.lk or with the Sri Lankan High Commission. Carry printed hotel vouchers and your return ticket anyway. Children's rules can differ, so check before applying for them.

Staying beyond 30 days needs a paid extension.

## Is 6 days enough for Sri Lanka?

It is enough for a first look, not for everything. Sri Lanka is a compact island, but the hill roads between Kandy and Nuwara Eliya are winding, and a few hours of driving can feel much longer than the map suggests. Six days gives you one cultural city, one hill stop and one beach stop, plus Colombo at either end.

What you give up is the rest of the island. This plan skips the ancient cities of the north-centre, the far south and the east coast. If you want those, plan eight to ten days instead, or save them for a second trip.

The plan works best with a private car and driver, which is also how most operator packages are built. You do not have to handle bus connections with luggage, and the driver knows where to stop for tea and viewpoints.

## How should you plan the 6-day route?

The route is Colombo, Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Bentota, then back to Colombo. Here is a version with a night in the cool hills.

| Day | Plan | Night |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Arrive Colombo, drive towards Kandy, stopping at Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage if time allows | Kandy |
| 2 | Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, Kandy town and lake, drive to hill country | Nuwara Eliya |
| 3 | Ramboda Falls, Hanuman temple, Seetha Amman Temple, tea country and town | Nuwara Eliya |
| 4 | Drive down to the coast, Madu River boat safari, beach time | Bentota |

| 6 | Airport transfer | – |

Exact road timings depend on traffic and weather. Hill roads are slow, so ask your operator for realistic drive times for each leg. Ask your operator for the sequence that fits your flight time. Day 1 and day 4 are the long driving days.

## What does each day feel like?

**Days 1–2: Colombo to Kandy.** Your first day is mostly a road day. Pinnawala's elephant orphanage can break the drive toward Kandy if you arrive early enough. In Kandy, the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic is the main sight, and the town sits around a lake, which makes an easy evening walk.

**Days 2–3: the hills.** The road climbs into tea country, and the air turns noticeably cooler. Ramboda Falls and the Hanuman temple at Ramboda sit on the way. Nuwara Eliya itself is a hill town surrounded by tea estates, and day 3 is deliberately light so you can see the Seetha Amman Temple without hurrying.

**Day 4: down to the coast.** This is the second long drive. You come down from the hills to Bentota, where the afternoon is for the Madu River boat ride through mangroves, or simply the beach.

**Day 5: Bentota to Colombo.** Kosgoda Turtle Hatchery is a short drive from Bentota. After that you head to Colombo for a city tour of colonial-era landmarks and shopping, and, if time allows, the Panchamuga Anjaneyar Temple.

## Which Ramayana sites can you visit?

Four sites are named in the sources. Whether you can fit all of them depends on your flight times and drive speeds, so ask your operator.

- **Munneswaram Temple**, a Shiva temple complex on the west side of the island, best added on the Colombo leg if time allows.
- **Sri Bhakta Hanuman Temple at Ramboda**, on the hill road, next to Ramboda Falls.
- **Seetha Amman Temple**, near Nuwara Eliya, which is linked to Ashok Vatika in the Ramayana story.
- **Panchamuga Anjaneyar Temple** in Colombo, a possible last-day stop.

Check temple timings locally, as some sites close or restrict entry at times. Dress modestly at all of them, and keep shoulders and knees covered. You will usually be asked to remove footwear, so wear shoes that are easy to slip off.

## What is worth doing in Kandy, Nuwara Eliya and Bentota?

**Kandy** is the cultural stop. The Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic is the main sight, and Pinnawala's elephant orphanage is a possible stop on the way from Colombo.

**Nuwara Eliya** is the hill stretch, with tea country and waterfalls like Ramboda. Carry a light jacket, as it is much cooler than the coast.

**Bentota** is for rest. A boat safari on the Madu River takes you through mangroves, the Kosgoda hatchery is a short drive, and the rest of your time is for the beach.

A scenic train from Kandy up to the hills is popular. Schedules and booking change, so ask us or check current options before building the plan around it.

## What should you check in a package before booking?

Two packages at a similar price can be very different. Before you pay, ask these questions:

- Which hotels are used each night, and in which town? A hotel in the hill town is very different from one an hour outside it.
- Are meals included, and which ones? This changes the real cost a lot.
- Is the vehicle private for the whole trip, and does the driver also act as guide?
- Are entrance fees included, or paid on the spot?
- Does the price include the ETA help, travel insurance and airport transfers?
- Is the price per person on twin sharing, and what is the rate for a child or a single traveller?

Discounted "from" prices in online listings can move, so ask for the final price in writing for your dates.

## What should you pack?

Pack for two climates. The coast is warm and humid, while Nuwara Eliya needs a light jacket. Bring modest clothes for temples, comfortable footwear that is easy to remove, sun protection for the beach and a small day bag for the boat safari. Keep your ETA approval, passport copy and hotel vouchers where you can reach them at the airport.

## What should you check before you go?

1. Apply for the ETA on eta.gov.lk and save the approval.
2. Check your passport validity and carry hotel and return-ticket proof.
3. Confirm payment options with your bank before travel.
4. Ask your operator for realistic drive times, since hill roads can be slow and are affected by rain.
