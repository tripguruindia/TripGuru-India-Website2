Most Delhi families head to Mussoorie or Nainital and find crowded roads and full parking. Uttarakhand has quieter options, and five of them are Chopta, Munsiyari, Kanatal, Binsar and Lansdowne. This guide helps you choose between them using the things that decide a trip: distance from Delhi, the season, and how much road you are willing to cover.

A note on what this article covers. The distances and seasons below come from travel-information pages, and sources do not always agree. We have not listed hotel rates or entry fees we could not confirm. Check current conditions before you book, or message us and we will help.

## Which offbeat Uttarakhand place suits which traveller?

| Place | Rough distance from Delhi | Best months (sources vary) | Suits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lansdowne | About 250–260 km | Spring and autumn | Short breaks, families |
| Kanatal | About 300 km | October–March | Easy weekend, cooler months |
| Chopta | About 350 km | March–May | Trekkers and mountain lovers |
| Binsar | Via Kathgodam and Almora | October–March | Forest and quiet stays |
| Munsiyari | Very long drive | November to mid-March | Travellers with 6+ days |

If you have a weekend, choose Lansdowne or Kanatal. If you have four to five days, add Binsar or Chopta. Munsiyari belongs on a longer trip.

## How to reach Lansdowne from Delhi

Lansdowne is the closest of the five. The road is about 250–260 km, going via Meerut, Bijnor and Kotdwar. The nearest railhead is Kotdwar, about 40 km from Lansdowne. From there you take a taxi or shared jeep.

Because Kotdwar is the only rail connection, many people drive from Delhi or Noida. If you prefer the train, check the current schedule on IRCTC. Best months are spring and autumn.

## How to reach Kanatal from Delhi

Kanatal is about 300 km from Delhi. There are two usual routes. One goes via Dehradun and Dhanaulti. The other goes via Rishikesh, Narendra Nagar and Chamba.

The route via Dehradun suits you if you are also stopping in Dehradun. The Rishikesh route suits you if you want a break at the Ganga first. Kanatal is best from October to March, according to the sources we read. Pack warm layers for winter evenings.

## How to reach Chopta from Delhi

Chopta is about 350 km from Delhi. One source puts this at 6–7 hours, but hill roads are slower than a map suggests. Plan for a full day of travel with stops, and do not count on arriving after dark.

Without a car, reach Haridwar or Rishikesh first. From there, buses and shared jeeps run towards Chopta. Confirm the current options locally.

The usual recommendation is March to May. Some sources point to winter as a different kind of season, so if you want snow, ask us or check recent local conditions before you commit. We could not confirm trek details such as the Tungnath–Chandrashila route from the sources we used, so check the trail and its season with a local operator.

## How to reach Binsar from Delhi

Binsar is reached through Almora. The nearest railhead is Kathgodam, and the road from there to Binsar via Almora is a journey of about 4 hours according to one source. Add the road time from Delhi to Kathgodam, and Binsar is better as an overnight stop than a one-day trip.

Sources give different best seasons. One says October to March. Another says February to April and October to November. October and November appear in both, which makes them the safest choice.

Binsar has a wildlife sanctuary. Entry fees, vehicle charges and timings change, so confirm them at the gate or with the forest department. Carry a photo ID.

## How to reach Munsiyari from Delhi

Munsiyari is the farthest of the five, and the long road is its main challenge. The nearest airport is Pantnagar. We do not have a reliable road distance beyond that, so we are not going to guess.

Plan an overnight stop on the way. Almora or the Kathgodam area are common choices. Message us and we can work out a day-by-day route. Sources suggest November to mid-March suits trekkers.

Munsiyari is worth the drive if you want a trip that feels far from the usual circuit. It is not worth it if you only have two or three days.

## What is the best time for offbeat Uttarakhand?

The five places do not share one season, so decide your month first.

1. **October–November:** Binsar and Kanatal are both in season. Lansdowne is good in autumn.
2. **December–February:** Kanatal, Binsar and Munsiyari are listed as suitable. Expect cold nights.
3. **March–May:** Chopta's main season. Lansdowne is good in spring.
4. **Monsoon:** Hill roads in Uttarakhand are prone to landslides in the monsoon. If you travel then, check road conditions on the day and keep your plan flexible.

## Which should you pick: Binsar or Kausani, Lansdowne or Kanatal?

Binsar and Kausani is a common question. We did not research Kausani, so we cannot compare them fairly. Ask us if you are choosing between them.

For Lansdowne and Kanatal, both suit a short drive from Delhi. Lansdowne is a little closer. Kanatal is on a route that links well to Dehradun and Rishikesh. If your group includes senior citizens, the shorter drive often matters more than anything else.

## How many days do you need?

Roughly:

- **Lansdowne or Kanatal:** a weekend of two nights works.
- **Binsar:** two to three nights, with Almora on the way.
- **Chopta:** three nights if you include a trek.
- **Munsiyari:** five to six days from Delhi, including the road.

These are our planning estimates, not figures from the sources.

## Can you combine these places into one trip?

Yes, but be realistic. Kanatal and Chopta are in the Garhwal hills, while Binsar and Munsiyari are in the Kumaon hills. Moving between the two regions takes a long day or more on the road.

A sensible plan is one region per trip. For example, go to Kanatal and Chopta in spring, or Binsar and Munsiyari in late autumn. Lansdowne can be added to either as a short stop only if the route suits you.

## What should you carry and check before you go?

- A valid photo ID for every adult, since protected areas often ask for it.
- Warm layers for winter. Rain cover in the monsoon.
- A full tank and some cash. Network and ATMs can be thin in the hills.
- Current road conditions on the day. Hill roads can be slow.
- Train and bus schedules on IRCTC or the operator's site.

We did not find any permit requirement for these five places, but confirm this locally before you set out.
