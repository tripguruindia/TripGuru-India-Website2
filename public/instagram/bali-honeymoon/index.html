Trip at a glance

Specially curated for couples

Beaches and cliff temples in the south, two slow days on the Gili Islands, then a private pool villa in the jungle of Ubud. Every day is planned for two, with time to relax together, not rush.

Stop 1South Bali3 nights

A beach base in Kuta for water sports, Uluwatu's cliff temple with the Kecak fire dance, and a day in the cool hills of North Bali.

Stop 2Gili2 nights

A fast boat to Gili Trawangan, where there are no cars, just bicycles, white sand and sunsets over the sea.

Stop 3Ubud3 nights

Your own private pool villa, the jungle swing and rice terraces, a Balinese massage and an ATV ride for two.

Day by day

Your 9 days in Bali

Tap a day to open it.
3 nights in KutaSouth Bali & the North
DAY01Arrive in BaliTransfers: Private carMeals: Breakfast from Day 2

Welcome to Bali. Our driver meets you at Denpasar airport and takes you to your beach resort in Kuta by private car. The rest of the day is free to rest, walk on the beach and watch your first Bali sunset together.

DAY02Water sports, Uluwatu and the Kecak fire dancePrivate transfersMeals: Breakfast

Start the morning with water sports: a banana boat ride, a jet ski and parasailing.

In the afternoon, drive to Uluwatu Temple on the cliffs above the Indian Ocean, then watch the Kecak fire dance at sunset.

DAY03North Bali: lake temple and Handara GatePrivate transfersEntry ticketsMeals: Breakfast

A full day in the cool green hills of the north. Visit Ulun Danu Beratan, the temple that floats on Lake Beratan, and take your photos at the famous Handara Gate. On the way back, relax at the Banjar hot springs.

2 nights on Gili TrawanganThe Gili Islands
DAY04Fast boat to the Gili IslandsPrivate car + fast boatMeals: Breakfast

After breakfast, a private car takes you to the harbour for your fast boat to Gili Trawangan. There are no cars on the island, so you get around on foot, by bicycle or by horse cart. Check in to your sea-facing bungalow and catch the sunset from the beach.

DAY05A slow day on Gili, just the two of youDay at leisureMeals: Breakfast

A free day to enjoy the island at your own pace. Cycle around the island, swim in clear water, find a quiet beach, and end the day with dinner by the sea.

DAY06Back to Bali and on to UbudFast boat + private carMeals: Breakfast

After breakfast, take the fast boat back to Bali. A private car drives you to Ubud and your private pool villa, with the jungle all around. The evening is yours.

3 nights in a private pool villaUbud
DAY07Ubud and KintamaniPrivate carEntry ticketsMeals: Breakfast

A full day with your own car and driver. See the Elephant Cave (Goa Gajah), fly over the valley on the jungle swing, visit Tegenungan Waterfall and the Tegallalang rice terraces, and enjoy the view of Mount Batur from Kintamani.

DAY08Balinese massage and an ATV ride for twoPrivate transfersMeals: Breakfast + Lunch

Relax with a 2-hour traditional Balinese massage. Then ride together on a tandem ATV for 90 minutes through jungle trails, rivers, rice fields and villages, with lunch included.

DAY09DepartureTransfers: Private carMeals: Breakfast

After breakfast, a private car takes you to the airport for your flight home.

What this plan covers, and what it does not

✓Covered in this plan

  • 8 nights: beach resort, island bungalow and private pool villa, with Breakfast every day.
  • Airport and hotel transfers by private car.
  • Fast boat to the Gili Islands and back, with car transfers to the harbour.
  • Water sports: banana boat, jet ski and parasailing.
  • Uluwatu Temple and the Kecak fire dance.
  • North Bali day: Ulun Danu, Handara Gate and Banjar hot springs.
  • Ubud and Kintamani day with jungle swing, waterfall and rice terraces.
  • 2-hour Balinese massage.
  • 90-minute tandem ATV ride with Lunch.

✕Not covered

  • International flights. We add these to your quote from your city.
  • Lunch and dinner, other than Lunch on the ATV day.
  • Visa and the Bali tourist levy.
  • Getting around on Gili (bicycle or horse cart).
  • Tips and personal expenses.

Good to know

  • There are no cars on the Gili Islands.
  • Before you fly, fill in Indonesia's online arrival and customs forms. We guide you step by step.
  • December to March is the green season, with short afternoon showers.
