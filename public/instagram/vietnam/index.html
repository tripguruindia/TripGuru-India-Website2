Three sides of Vietnam in ten days

This plan covers the mountains and old towns of central Vietnam, three nights on Phu Quoc island, and day trips from Hanoi to Ninh Binh and Ha Long Bay. The days are balanced, with guided sightseeing, theme parks and free time by the sea.

Day by day

Your 10 days in Vietnam

Tap a day to open it.
3 nights in Da NangDa Nang & Hoi An
DAY01Arrive in Da NangPrivate car transferMeals: Not included today

Welcome to Vietnam. Our driver meets you at Da Nang airport and takes you to your hotel by private car. The rest of the day is free to rest or take a walk by the beach. Hotel check-in is from 2:00 PM.

DAY02Ba Na Hills and the Golden BridgePrivate tourWith guideMeals: Breakfast

Your guide picks you up at about 8:30 AM. Ride the cable car up to Ba Na Hills and walk across the Golden Bridge, held up by two giant stone hands. Visit the French Village, the flower gardens and the hilltop viewpoints, and take the funicular train.

In the afternoon, enjoy the free games at Fantasy Park. You are back at your hotel by about 5:00 PM, and the evening is free.

Optional, at extra cost: Alpine coaster, Wax Museum and the wine cellar at Ba Na Hills.

DAY03Hoi An Ancient Town and lantern boat ridePrivate tourWith guideMeals: Breakfast

The morning is free. At about 3:30 PM, your guide takes you to Hoi An. Walk through the old town to the Japanese Covered Bridge, the assembly halls and the old merchant houses, then stroll along the lantern streets as the lights come on.

A lantern boat ride on the Thu Bon River is included. You return to your hotel in Da Nang by about 9:00 PM.

3 nights in Phu QuocPhu Quoc Island
DAY04Fly to Phu Quoc and evening at Grand WorldPrivate car transferMeals: Breakfast

After breakfast, a private car takes you to Da Nang airport for your flight to Phu Quoc. On arrival, you are met and driven to your hotel.

In the evening, visit Grand World, the Venice-style entertainment district. Walk along the canal and watch the free water, light and music show at the Lake of Love.

Optional, at extra cost: Gondola ride, the Quintessence of Vietnam show and the Teddy Bear Museum.

DAY05VinWonders and Vinpearl SafariPrivate car transferFastpass entryMeals: Breakfast

A full day at two parks on one combined ticket. Pick-up is at about 10:00 AM. Your Fastpass gives you priority entry at both parks, and electric buggies take you around inside. You are dropped back at your hotel by about 6:00 PM.

DAY06Hon Thom cable car and sea showsPrivate tourWith guideMeals: Breakfast

Your guide takes you to the south of the island for the Hon Thom cable car, one of the world's longest cable car rides over the sea, with views of the islands below. In the evening, watch the Symphony of the Sea and Kiss the Sea shows and walk on the Kiss Bridge.

3 nights in HanoiHanoi, Ninh Binh & Ha Long Bay
DAY07Fly to HanoiPrivate car transferMeals: Breakfast

After breakfast, a private car takes you to Phu Quoc airport for your flight to Hanoi. On arrival, you are met and driven to your hotel. The evening is free to explore the city.

DAY08Ninh Binh day tourWith guideMeals: Breakfast

Your guide picks you up from your Hanoi hotel in the morning for the drive to Ninh Binh, about two hours each way. Visit Hoa Lu, the ancient capital of Vietnam, then take a rowing boat ride at Tam Coc through rice fields and limestone caves.

Climb the steps to the Mua Cave viewpoint for wide views over the valley. You return to Hanoi in the evening.

DAY09Ha Long Bay 5-star day cruiseShared coach and cruiseWith guideMeals: Breakfast + Indian Lunch

Pick-up is at about 8:00 AM for the drive to Ha Long Bay by expressway. Board your cruise around noon and sail for about five hours among the limestone islands, with an Indian Lunch served on board.

Visit Sung Sot (Surprise) Cave, go kayaking or take a bamboo boat at Luon Cave, and stop at Titop Island for the beach and viewpoint. Enjoy tea and cakes on the deck at sunset, and return to your Hanoi hotel by about 9:00 PM.

DAY10DeparturePrivate car transferMeals: Breakfast

After breakfast, a private car takes you to Hanoi airport for your flight home, with ten days of Vietnam to remember.
